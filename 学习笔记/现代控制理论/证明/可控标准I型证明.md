$$
由$\overline{A}=T_{c1}^{-1}AT_{c1}$得到：
$$
T_{c1}\overline{A}=AT_{c1}=[Ae_{1},Ae_{2},...,Ae_{n}]=[A^{n}b,A^{n-1}b,\cdots,Ab]\begin{bmatrix}1&0&\cdots&\cdots&0\\\alpha_{n-1}&1&\ddots&&\vdots\\\vdots&\ddots&\ddots&\ddots&\vdots\\\alpha_{2}&&\ddots&\ddots&0\\\alpha_{1}&\alpha_{2}&\cdots&\alpha_{n-1}&1\end{bmatrix}(*)
$$
展开后对应向量相等
$$
\begin{aligned}Ae_{1}&=A^{n}b+\alpha_{n-1}A^{n-1}b+\cdots+\alpha_{1}Ab\\&=A^{n}b+\alpha_{n-1}A^{n-1}b+\cdots+\alpha_{1}Ab+\alpha_{0}b-\alpha_{0}b\\&=[A^{n}+\alpha_{n-1}A^{n-1}+\cdots+\alpha_{1}A+\alpha_{0}]b-\alpha_{0}b\end{aligned}
$$

对于一个矩阵的特征多项式
$$
p(\lambda)=\lambda^n+c_{n-1}\lambda^{n-1}+\cdots+c_1\lambda+c_0
$$

$$
p(A)=A^n+c_{n-1}A^{n-1}+\cdots+c_1A+c_0I=0
$$

$$
Ae_1=-\alpha_0b=-\alpha_0e_n
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}Ae_{2}&=A^{n-1}b+\alpha_{n-1}A^{n-2}b+\cdots+\alpha_{2}Ab+\alpha_{1}b-\alpha_{1}b\\&=e_1-\alpha_1e_n\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}&Ae_{n-1}=A^{2}b+\alpha_{n-1}Ab+\alpha_{n-2}b-\alpha_{n-2}b=e_{n-2}-\alpha_{n-2}e_{n}\\&Ae_{n}=Ab+\alpha_{n-1}b-\alpha_{n-1}b=e_{n-1}-\alpha_{n-1}e_{n}\end{aligned}
$$
